CSX texture streaming was developed with reference to SPS. The reference
covers distance-based demand, GPU copies of lower mip levels, a restoration
RAM buffer, adjustable VRAM reserve, remembered load sizes and a size cap.
Preview protection and urgency-ranked restoration also reference SPS.
CSX idle inventories reuse unchanged demand until a bounded refresh.
Reduced textures receive service before new demand.

SPS's src/TextureStream.cpp also documents the DDS-loader ABI and its
SE/AE/VR locations (75721, 77533, 0xDD2B60), and the texture-creation slot
of BSShaderResourceManager (0xD0 / 8 = 0x1A). These integration details are
acknowledged at the corresponding CSX hook sites.

CSX shadow culling adapts the sun-angle and point-light magnification
policy from SPS src/ShadowCulling.cpp, modified on 2026-10-28. Ordinary
caster thresholds and nearest-cascade protection reference that policy.
Distance defaults are rounded to 20/15/50 metres for sun/torch/NPCs. CSX adds
retained camera snapshots, stereo checks, actor-root distance protection
and independent Light Limit Fix settings.
SPS 1.0.70's point-light collection fix also informed CSX's draw-only rejection;
CSX uses its existing native light scopes and shared render-pass hooks.

CSX has its own streaming policy, stereo demand calculation, validated DDS
reader, bounded restoration cache, remembered-size format, upload and GPU
retirement code. SPS binaries and assets are not bundled. The adapted
shadow policy is marked at its CSX source location.
