Version csx3.19.2-VR
[Changelog since CSX3.18]

[VR Render Scale and Upscaling] Switching between DLSS, FSR and native rendering is more reliable, including repeated or rapid profile changes. (by: CSX)
[VR Render Scale and Upscaling] Saved Render Scale settings are restored more reliably at startup, including native-rendering fallback when the requested upscaler is unavailable. (by: CSX)
[VR Render Scale and Upscaling] Door transitions, fast travel and save loading now handle resolution changes more safely, reducing white frames, stretched images, VR mask artefacts and black-screen hangs. (by: CSX)
[VR Render Scale and Upscaling] Failed or interrupted resolution changes recover more safely, retain the last working presentation where possible and avoid repeated recovery loops. Ordinary saves and missing camera history no longer leave rendering stuck in recovery. (by: CSX)
[VR Render Scale and Upscaling] FSR3/FSR4 fallback, sharpening settings and stereo dispatch are handled more consistently during profile changes and recovery. (by: CSX)
[VR Render Scale and Upscaling] Added an optional link between Render Scale and DLSS/FSR selections, preserving the user's preference when switching through native anti-aliasing modes. (by: CSX)
[VR Render Scale and Upscaling] Added optional FSR3/FSR4 temporal tuning for motion response, reactivity, shading changes and history accumulation, with provider defaults retained when tuning is disabled or rejected. (by: CSX)
[VR Render Scale and Upscaling] Added optional motion-adaptive RCAS and Luma Unsharp sharpening for DLSS/DLAA, allowing sharpness to respond to head and camera movement. (by: CSX)
[VR Render Scale and Upscaling] Foveated mask previews work again, including full-coverage settings. Enabling Foveated Upscaling now selects compatible SSGI and Screen Space Shadows foveation defaults while preserving individual opt-outs. (by: CSX)
[VR Render Scale and Upscaling] VR depth-culling controls, defaults and recovery have been revised to handle startup, loading and frame-history changes more reliably. (by: CSX)
[VR Render Scale and Upscaling] Closing the VR map now releases its retained menu image, addressing map controls remaining over gameplay when resolution upscaling is enabled. (by: CSX)

[VR Presets] AMD and NVIDIA systems now share GPU-unified Performance, Balanced and Quality preset tiers with consistent settings and compatibility checks. (by: CSX)
[VR Presets] Updated the shared preset appearance with revised volumetric lighting, Fantasy bloom, skin and hair settings; IBL is disabled in these supplied presets. (by: CSX)
[VR Presets] Retuned MGO preset lighting for interiors, daytime and nighttime, with detailed profile controls enabled so the intended adjustments take effect. (by: CSX)

[Adaptive Balance and Bloom] Added Global, interior, time-of-day and location-specific Lighting, Bloom and Water profiles, with smooth transitions and consistent setting layers. (by: CSX)
[Adaptive Balance and Bloom] Location settings can supplement or replace their inherited profile, with existing profile settings migrated to the unified layout. (by: CSX)
[Adaptive Balance and Bloom] Bloom profiles work independently of unrelated Linear Lighting controls, and detailed bloom shaping no longer adds unintended strength. (by: CSX)
[Adaptive Balance and Bloom] Added profile-aware Sky Saturation controls alongside Sky Brightness. (by: OS + CSX)
[Adaptive Balance and Bloom] The Ambient control now adjusts the final vanilla or image-based ambient lighting consistently, including reflections, without applying the adjustment twice. (by: CSX)
[Adaptive Balance and Bloom] The master enable switch correctly restores neutral lighting when disabled and retains saved adjustments for re-enabling. Essential and Advanced controls are more consistent. (by: CSX)

[Unified Water and Wetness] Added advanced controls for water brightness, reflections, refraction, sun specular, waves, Fresnel limits and muddiness, integrated with Adaptive Balance profiles. (by: OS + CSX)
[Unified Water and Wetness] Added wind-driven wave behaviour, with separate base wave amplitude and calm/strong-wind controls while preserving existing weather-aware settings. (by: CSX)
[Unified Water and Wetness] Added profile-aware caustics strength, tiling, animation speed and colour dispersion, plus water-parallax strength and quality controls. Saved and imported water profiles now retain these parallax and caustics controls. (by: OS + CSX)
[Unified Water and Wetness] Water appearance settings retain a working fallback when Unified Water is unavailable or disabled. Failed flowmap loading is handled more safely. (by: CSX)
[Unified Water and Wetness] Fixed excessive IBL darkening in refracted water, improved flowmap mipmapping and restored animated normals for blended water. Invalid refraction depth and colour values are handled more safely. (by: CS + OS + CSX)
[Unified Water and Wetness] Added Simple, Textured, Textured High Quality and Legacy Procedural puddle-mask modes, with cached noise for the textured modes. (by: CSX)
[Unified Water and Wetness] Added adjustable rain darkening for ordinary and complex grass, following the existing drying cycle. (by: CSX + OS)
[Unified Water and Wetness] Water objects unloaded with a cell now release orphaned reflection and normal entries, addressing crashes caused by stale water materials. (by: CS + CSX)

[Screenshots] Added versioned screenshot and image-sequence APIs for companion mods and automation, including headset-image capture and capture receipts. (by: CSX)
[Screenshots] Still-image and sequence capture now honour their respective eye, encoding and destination settings more reliably, with safer request handling and cleanup. (by: CSX)

[Shader Cache and Compilation] Replaced loose shader-cache storage with managed, compatibility-checked packs and safe fallback to compilation when a pack cannot be used. (by: CSX)
[Shader Cache and Compilation] AIO cache packaging, Mod Organizer 2 installation and runtime-specific cache selection are more reliable, including portable Horizon Fix profiles and a manual FOMOD cache installer. Corrected SE/AE prebuilt-cache input mismatches that caused unnecessary shader recompilation. (by: CSX + OS)
[Shader Cache and Compilation] Warm-cache reuse, cache clearing and background compilation now handle pending work more safely, preventing stale writes and unnecessary recompilation. (by: OS + CSX)
[Shader Cache and Compilation] Improved compilation responsiveness and in-game compiler priority, with sortable compilation statistics in the menu. (by: OS + CSX)
[Shader Cache and Compilation] Changing background compilation mode now wakes the compiler immediately. A failed cache-file lookup can fall back to source compilation. (by: CS + CSX)
[Shader Cache and Compilation] Shader category toggles now control the intended shaders, including ImageSpace compute shaders that previously followed the Lighting toggle. (by: CS + CSX)
[Shader Cache and Compilation] Shader includes now load without consuming the limited C-runtime file slots, preventing compilation failures caused by file-table exhaustion. (by: OS + CSX)
[Shader Cache and Compilation] Cache packaging now rejects malformed shader bytecode and entries for the wrong shader stage. (by: OS + CSX)

[Lighting and Visual Fixes] Skylighting now supports per-probe shadows, with more reliable shadow-history updates, refreshes after loading and safer capture-state restoration. (by: CS + OS + CSX)
[Lighting and Visual Fixes] Terrain shadow edges are more stable, with corrected height propagation, pixel alignment and soft-shadow interpolation. (by: CS + CSX)
[Lighting and Visual Fixes] Added shared foliage scattering controls for trees and grass, including a runtime toggle. Backlit foliage now respects the available screen-space shadows. (by: OS + CSX)
[Lighting and Visual Fixes] Landscape-textured meshes can use Terrain Variation, with eligibility restricted to registered landscape textures during normal gameplay. (by: CS + OS + CSX)
[Lighting and Visual Fixes] Added a Performance/Quality selector for dynamic cubemaps, incorporating the higher-resolution capture option. (by: CSX + CS)
[Lighting and Visual Fixes] Restored working godray saturation and custom-colour controls for interiors and exteriors, including bright HDR weather colours. (by: CSX)
[Lighting and Visual Fixes] Added a Sky Sync control for weather lens flares. (by: CSX)
[Lighting and Visual Fixes] Corrected additive fog lighting, non-Linear-Lighting reflection normalization and skin darkening with Linear Lighting. Fog and reflection sampling are better isolated between VR eyes. (by: CS + OS + CSX)
[Lighting and Visual Fixes] TruePBR LOD, projected and mislinked materials are handled more safely, and native grass bounds have been corrected. (by: CS + OS + CSX)

[Editors, Settings and Menu] Reworked the Lighting Editor with mesh picking, live light overrides, Light Placer add/edit/delete workflows, filtering, shadow bias and emittance controls. (by: CS + CSX)
[Editors, Settings and Menu] Lighting Editor selection, VR pointer mapping and saved edits are handled more safely across reloads and editor shutdown. (by: CS + CSX)
[Editors, Settings and Menu] Desktop menu movement, docking, resizing and custom headset placement are available as persistent options. (by: CSX)
[Editors, Settings and Menu] The native Skyrim UI pointer remains visible above CSX menus when using VR Menu Mouse Fix. (by: CSX)
[Editors, Settings and Menu] The Upscaling menu has clearer profile and Render Scale controls, plus a direct link that opens VR settings on the FOV tab. (by: CSX)
[Editors, Settings and Menu] Reduced menu input lag and preserved finer mouse-wheel scrolling. Fixed flat-screen welcome-text flicker and menu movement around status panels. (by: CS + CSX)
[Editors, Settings and Menu] Settings saves can fall back to direct writes when file replacement fails, and incompatible-plugin messages explain why a plugin is blocked. (by: OS + CSX)

[Performance Tools] Expanded feature-cost controls and added automated upscaling comparisons, with closed-menu measurement, settling periods and restoration of the original settings. (by: CSX)
[Performance Tools] CPU/GPU and A/B profiling now avoid nested double-counting, stale timings and mixed sample windows, with clearer reporting of missing measurements. (by: CS + OS + CSX)
[Performance Tools] Restored CPU/GPU timing for SE/AE's D3D11 presentation path, alongside the existing VR timing support. (by: CSX)

[Mod Integration] Added versioned APIs for upscaling, weather, shaders, profiling, editor access and feature controls, allowing companion mods to use supported interfaces. (by: CSX)
[Mod Integration] Added OCU integration for accepted-draw masking and optional gaze-driven AO/GI sampling, with native sampling retained when the optional gaze data is unavailable or invalid. (by: CSX)

[Compatibility and Stability] VR shadow rendering now protects scene lights and sun ownership and prevents cyclic shadow submissions that could freeze the game. (by: CSX)
[Compatibility and Stability] Grass instance groups are protected while culling and cell unloading overlap, addressing a VR crash caused by freed grass data. (by: CSX)
[Compatibility and Stability] Added protection against exhausted native culling pools, while respecting fixes already provided by Engine Fixes. Light Limit Fix compatibility with Engine Fixes VR has also improved. (by: OS + CSX)
[Compatibility and Stability] Missing material textures, invalid lighting draws, malformed TruePBR data, missing skin keywords and failed graphics hooks are handled more safely. (by: CS + OS + CSX)
[Compatibility and Stability] Reduced repeated CPU bookkeeping in material checks and shadow submissions while retaining lifetime and duplicate-submission protection. (by: CSX)
[Compatibility and Stability] Fixed unsafe reuse of DirectX 12 command allocators in the SE/AE frame-generation path. (by: CS + CSX)
[Compatibility and Stability] When RenderDoc is enabled or loaded, CSX now selects SteamVR compositor menus and suppresses incompatible Stabilizer synchronization and post-load presentation holds. (by: CSX)
