Thread execution and scheduler time are not fpsVR CPU frame time. Process sampled execution sums concurrent threads. Stack contexts overlap; do not sum them as exclusive costs or interpret wrapper-inclusive samples as hook overhead. A zero means no matching samples under the preserved classifier, not proof that a mechanism is free or absent. Different symbol names in the RC166 source may not match newer guards.
