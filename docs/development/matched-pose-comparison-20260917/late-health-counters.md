# Late-hold freshness counters

These are deltas between the saved approximately +49-second and stop health samples. Their exact observed times are retained in JSON. They span about eleven seconds and are supporting evidence, not substitutes for the fixed [50,60) fpsVR/WPR windows. Process-lifetime totals include menus/loading; only monotonic deltas are compared. Atomic counters are sampled independently, so small endpoint differences are possible.

These counters describe submit-stage render-scale work. Native DLAA executes outside this submit-stage path; its zero submit-stage vendor count does not mean DLAA was disabled. One vendor evaluation per eye is expected: two eye attempts per complete stereo frame are not duplicate inference. Zero reuse hits do not justify overwriting the colour-managed prepared input. The separate cpuPerformance counters are inactive/reset where indicated; their zeros are unavailable instrumentation, not zero CPU cost.
